# India crowd test set

21 photos of crowds in India: Kumbh Mela (Prayagraj), railway stations
(Thane, Mumbai, Dum Dum), Rath Yatra (Puri), Ganesh festival (Mumbai), Durga
Puja and Chhath (Kolkata), Sabarimala, temple queues and processions.

They test whether Crowd Shield's counter, trained on ShanghaiTech, works on the
crowds it was built for. Every photo is freely licensed on Wikimedia Commons;
`sources.csv` lists each one's photographer, licence and source page. The
images here were resized to at most 1920 px wide. Photos under CC BY-SA keep
that licence.

Head positions were marked by a person: the model's suggested points were
loaded into an annotation tool and every photo was checked and corrected by
hand, adding missed heads and removing wrong points.
