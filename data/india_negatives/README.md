# Indian railway tracks with no people

23 freely licensed photos (Wikimedia Commons; credits in `sources.csv`) of
Indian railway tracks, ballast and platforms with nobody in them, each checked
by eye. They teach the counter that track gravel is not a crowd: on a Mumbai
station photo the original model marked hundreds of "heads" on the ballast.
They are used for training only, with a true count of zero, never for testing.
