# Trying Crowd Shield with real people

Crowd Shield has not been used at a real event yet. This is a 15-minute test anyone can run with
someone who works with crowds, to find out what is confusing or missing. Results go in a table at the
bottom; only real sessions belong there.

## Before you start
- Ask for consent: "I'm testing a student project. I'll note what you do, not who you are. You can stop any time."
- Don't record faces or names. Note only the role (organiser, security, student...).
- Open the app on their device, or share your screen: https://crowd-shield-india.streamlit.app
- Use the **sample photos** and the **Demo feed**; the live server is slow on a new photo.

## Tasks (say them out loud, then stay quiet and watch)
1. "Find out how many people are in this photo." (Count people in a photo, sample photo)
2. "Which zone at the Kumbh site is in the most danger, and why?" (Site map)
3. "If that zone is full, where would you send people?" (Site map, evacuation plan)
4. "How would you know if a crowd is building up on a camera?" (Watch a live camera, demo)
5. "How would you want to be warned?" (Settings, alert channels)

For each task note: did they finish, how long it took, where they hesitated, anything they said out loud.

## Questions afterwards
1. What would you use this for? Would you use it at your events or sites?
2. Which number or screen did you trust least, and why?
3. What is missing before you would use it for real?
4. How easy was it, 1 (very hard) to 5 (very easy)?
5. Would you act on its count? No / only with a person checking / as an extra signal / yes.

They can also fill in the feedback form: https://github.com/khushi-git-7/Crowd_shield/issues/new?template=feedback.yml
